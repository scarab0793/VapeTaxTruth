THE REAL COST OF THE UK VAPE TAX

THE VAPE TAX.
WHAT IT COSTS YOU.

From 1 October 2026, UK Vaping Products Duty charges 22p per millilitre of covered vape liquid — whether or not it contains nicotine. For people who have switched away from cigarettes, the extra cost can be substantial. Here is how the figures stack up, what ministers have said and what the tobacco industry stands to gain or lose.

POLICY & INDUSTRY

Tax, tobacco and vested interests.

The tobacco industry has a commercial interest in both cigarettes and vaping. Its involvement in tax discussions deserves public scrutiny, particularly where independent retailers and former smokers face higher costs.

DOCUMENTED LAW

A flat rate for all nicotine levels

The Finance Act 2026 sets duty at £2.20 per 10 ml and covers nicotine-free liquid intended for vaping.

Additional business rates burden

From April 2027, vape shops in England lose eligibility for specified retail and small-business rates reliefs. The official costing anticipates £10 million annually in additional Exchequer revenue from the combined measure covering vape shops and adult gaming centres, not vape shops alone.

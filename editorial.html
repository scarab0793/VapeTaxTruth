About VapeTaxTruth

VapeTaxTruth is an independent campaign and information site about the UK's Vaping Products Duty. We think former smokers who switched to vaping, and the small businesses that serve them, deserve clear numbers on what the tax costs, and that the tobacco industry's commercial interests deserve public scrutiny.

We have a clear point of view, and we keep it separate from the facts. This page explains how.

Editorial policy

  • Facts come from primary sources: legislation, HMRC and GOV.UK publications, OBR and Treasury documents, Companies House, and companies' own statements. Each is linked.
  • Forecasts are labelled as forecasts and kept apart from published receipts.
  • Opinion is labelled as opinion.
  • Tobacco industry links. We report documented meetings, ownership and company statements. A meeting or a shared owner shows contact or a commercial interest, not that a company wrote the policy. We do not claim more than the record shows.
  • Closures and job losses. We count a closure only when a dated, sourced statement or filing exists. A shop announcing it will close is recorded as announced, not completed. When the number of jobs lost is not published we say it is unknown. It is never recorded as zero, and estimates are never added to confirmed figures.
  • Community posts and reports from readers are unverified personal accounts, not evidence of national trends.
  • Health. Vaping is not risk-free. We discuss it as a lower-risk alternative to smoking, as official bodies do, and do not suggest that non-smokers should start.

Funding and independence

The site has no advertising and sells nothing. It runs on free hosting.

Corrections

If something here is wrong, tell us through the report form by choosing "Other evidence" and giving the page, the claim and a source. We will check it, fix confirmed errors promptly, and note significant corrections on the page they affect.

Contact

The report form is the way to reach us. Reports go to a private register that only the site operator can read.

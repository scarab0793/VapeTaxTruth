What this site is

VapeTaxTruth is an independent information project about UK Vaping Products Duty. It is not a shop, it does not sell or promote vaping products, and it is not affiliated with HMRC, any political party or any tobacco or vaping company.

Estimates are not advice

The calculator is a scenario model. It uses the statutory rate of £2.20 per 10 ml and assumptions you can change. Real shelf prices depend on retailers, existing stock and how a product is classified. It is not a retailer quote, tax advice or a forecast.

Reports you send us

Send only information you believe is true and that you are entitled to share. Do not include personal data, abuse or allegations you cannot source. We may decline, edit or not publish any report.

Using our content

You are welcome to quote and link to this site. Please credit VapeTaxTruth and link to the page you used. Linked sources belong to their publishers.

Accuracy

We check facts against primary sources and say when something is an estimate or an open question. If you spot a mistake, see our corrections policy.

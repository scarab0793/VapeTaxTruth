Privacy policy

Last updated 27 November 2026.

VapeTaxTruth is an independent public-interest project. This page explains what happens to the information you send us and what we do not collect.

Who is responsible for your data

The data controller is VAPETAXTRUTH, the independent campaign and information project that runs this site. You can reach us through the report form on the home page.

What we collect

Only what you type into the "Tell us your story" form: the type of report, an optional business or location, your description, and an optional public source link. We do not ask for your name, email address or phone number. Please do not include personal details about yourself or other people. The form also records the time the report was received.

Why we collect it

To review reports about shop closures, price rises and other effects of Vaping Products Duty, and to publish a report only once it has been checked. Nothing is published automatically.

Where it is stored and who can see it

Reports are saved in a private Google spreadsheet that only the site operator can open. Google processes the data on our behalf. The spreadsheet is never made public. If a report is published, it is rewritten without personal or identifying details and linked to a public source where one exists.

How long we keep it

Reports that are rejected or are duplicates are deleted within 30 days. Reports that are approved but not published are kept for no more than 12 months. A published report stays on the site, but only in a form with personal and identifying details removed. If you ask us to delete a report, we will do so within 30 days.

Visitors

This site does not use cookies, advertising trackers or analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs such as IP addresses under GitHub's privacy statement. Your browser remembers the time of your last report for one minute, to stop accidental double submissions. That value stays on your device.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018 you can ask to see, correct or delete personal data we hold about you. Because reports are anonymous, we can only act on a request if you quote enough of your report for us to find it. Send the request through the report form, choosing "Other evidence". If you are unhappy with how we handle your data you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.
